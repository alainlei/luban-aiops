Guides assume a Makefile-driven workflow (`make sync`, `make build`, `make deploy`) orchestrated through Kustomize overlays under `shared/platform-ops/gitops/`; deployment targets a Kubernetes cluster (kind/minikube/real) and provisions secrets via helper scripts such as `sync-runtime-secret.sh`, `sync-delegation-secrets.sh`, and `sync-skills-secrets.sh`.
