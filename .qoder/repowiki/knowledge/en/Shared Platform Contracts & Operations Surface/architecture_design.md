Two sibling concerns sit side-by-side: `shared-contracts/` owns the source-of-truth wire formats (JSON Schemas under `schemas/`, YAML policy bundles under `policies/`) and their validation scripts; `platform-ops/` consumes them — its GitOps manifests (`dev-k8s/`) and runtime-profile overlays declare services whose request/response payloads must match those schemas, and its e2e demo scripts exercise the same endpoints defined by the contracts. `base-images/base-uv/Dockerfile` provides the common Python/uv runtime image referenced by both children's deployments. There is no code import between the two children; the contract files themselves are the binding artifact.
