Flat collection of standalone POSIX shell scripts, each targeting one product slice (documents, http-check tools, incidents, mutations, secrets, skills, observability) plus one disposable Python-based failure harness. Every script is self-contained: it defines its own `fail`, `json_field`, HTTP helpers, and token-exchange flow rather than sharing code. Two execution modes coexist:
- Cluster-facing demos (`documents-demo.sh`, `http-check-demo.sh`, `incident-demo.sh`, `mutating-demo.sh`, `secret-delivery-demo.sh`, `skills-demo.sh`) assume `make deploy` has run, use `kubectl port-forward` for `platform-gateway` (18083) and `identity-service` (18081), and exercise REST endpoints under `/api/v1/*`.
- The `observability-livecheck.sh` entry point dispatches between a mocked pytest leg (`--local`) and a read-only cluster preflight (`--live`) that probes OTEL env vars, `/metrics`, and OpenObserve; an optional paid turn gated by `LUBAN_OBS_DRIVE_PAID_TURN=1` drives one billable LLM call and correlates trace_id in OpenObserve.
- `execution-failure-test.sh` is deliberately isolated from the cluster: it locates the repo root, requires `docker`/`uv`, and runs `products/execution-runtime/tests/failure/run.py --stage campaign` inside the project's uv environment, optionally wrapped in `caffeinate` to avoid host-sleep clock skew.
Dependency direction is one-way outward: scripts depend on the running services but have no shared library — they are leaf fixtures consumed by CI or developers after deployment.
