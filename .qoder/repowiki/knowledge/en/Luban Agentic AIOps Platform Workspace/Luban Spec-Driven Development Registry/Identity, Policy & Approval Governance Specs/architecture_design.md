Three independent SPEC documents under `docs/specs/`, each following a uniform plan/spec/tasks triad that drives implementation across product boundaries:
- SPEC-004 (Deny-By-Default Policy Enforcement) introduces the shared policy contract in `shared/shared-contracts` (`policy-rule.schema.json`, `policy-decision.schema.json`, `policies/policy-default.yaml`) and an in-memory evaluation module inside `products/tool-gateway`; it is deliberately dependency-free and future-proofs a lift-and-shift to `policy-center`.
- SPEC-008 (Service-to-Service Identity) implements ADR-0004: `identity-broker` exposes `POST /api/v1/auth/exchange` to mint audience-bound delegated tokens; `tool-gateway` caches them per-user subject and forwards them as bearer tokens to `agent-platform`, which strips identity from request bodies and carries it only via `Authorization`.
- SPEC-030 (Require-Approval Policy Semantics) extends SPEC-004 by activating the reserved `require_approval` outcome with two tiers (`tier_1` self-approval allowed, `tier_2` requires a distinct approver), bridging enforcement onto the existing `chat:confirm` HITL substrate without introducing a queue service.
Dependency direction is one-way: specs reference product code paths but are not consumed at runtime — they are living contracts whose acceptance criteria drive tests, overlays, and the default policy bundle. All three are marked `delivered` and frozen.
