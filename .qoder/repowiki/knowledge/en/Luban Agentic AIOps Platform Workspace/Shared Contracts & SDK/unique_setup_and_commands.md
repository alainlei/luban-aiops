Policy bundle rollout follows SPEC-048: edit only `policies/policy-default.yaml`, bump its `version`, then run `make sync-policy` to propagate packaged copies and the dev-k8s ConfigMap source; `make verify` enforces `policy-scenarios.yaml`; `make policy-diff CANDIDATE=<bundle>` reports per-(role, action) outcome transitions at review time. Skill documents are linted locally via `python -m skills_hub.validate <directory>` before publishing.
