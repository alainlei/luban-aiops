`make verify` is the canonical pre-commit/pre-push gate running product tests, kustomize overlay renders, dashboard validation, policy/schema/version/secret-vocabulary/password-policy checks, secret-delivery demo, portal Vitest + production build, and the crash-safety failure test. `make build` produces a coordinated IMAGE_TAG across all products and writes `.images.env`; `make deploy` applies the dev-k8s overlay; `make e2e` runs the full suite of demo scripts against a deployed cluster after port-forwarding gateway and identity-service.
