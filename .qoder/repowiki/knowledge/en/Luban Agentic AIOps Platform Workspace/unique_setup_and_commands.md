Root entry points: `make verify` (pre-commit/pre-push gate running all product tests, Kustomize render checks, dashboard validation, policy validation/scenarios/diff, version lockstep, secret-vocabulary check, password-policy check, secret-delivery demo, and portal vitest+build), `make build` (coordinated image tag written to `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`), `make deploy` (wraps `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh`), `make e2e` (runs skill/incident/mutating/http-check/secret-delivery/observability-livecheck/acme-admin demos), `make observability-livecheck` (mocked by default, `LIVE=1` for read-only cluster pre-flight, `DRIVE_TURN=1` for gated billable one-turn correlation), and `make deploy-samples` / `make deploy-sample-app` for out-of-band sample installation.
