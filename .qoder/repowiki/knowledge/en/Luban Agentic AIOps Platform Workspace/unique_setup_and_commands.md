`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate aggregating product tests, overlay render, dashboard validation, policy/scenario checks, version lockstep, secret vocabulary, secret-delivery demo, portal unit+build, and execution-failure proof. `make build` produces all product images with one coordinated tag and writes `.images.env`; `make deploy` applies the dev-k8s Kustomize overlay; `make e2e` runs the full demo suite against a live cluster after port-forwarding platform-gateway and identity-service.
