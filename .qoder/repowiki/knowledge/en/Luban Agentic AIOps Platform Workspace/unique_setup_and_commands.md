`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate running every product test suite, kustomize overlay renders, dashboard validation, policy scenario checks, version/secret-vocabulary/password-policy locks, secret-delivery demo, portal Vitest + tsc build, and the crash-safety failure test. `make build` produces a coordinated IMAGE_TAG applied to all nine container images and writes `.images.env`; `make deploy` applies the `dev-k8s` overlay; `make e2e` runs the full demo suite against a live cluster after port-forwarding `platform-gateway` and `identity-service`.
