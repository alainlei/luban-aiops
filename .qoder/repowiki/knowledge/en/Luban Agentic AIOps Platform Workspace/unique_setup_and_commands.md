Root `make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate combining every product test suite, Kustomize overlay rendering, canonical policy validation, scenario evaluation, version lockstep check, secret-vocabulary validation, password-policy validation, secret-delivery demo, and the portal Vitest + production build. Per-product commands are invoked via `make -C products/<name> help`. Coordinated image builds write `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env` consumed by `make deploy`, which runs `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh`. E2E demos require port-forwards to `platform-gateway:18083` and `identity-service:18081` before `make e2e`.
