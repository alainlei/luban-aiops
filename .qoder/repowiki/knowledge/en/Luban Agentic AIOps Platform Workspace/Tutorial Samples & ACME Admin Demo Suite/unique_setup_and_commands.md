`make deploy-sample-app` builds the image, applies `acme-admin/deploy/`, and asserts healthz/hello/users endpoints; `make deploy-samples` installs all skill docs into the `skills-samples` ConfigMap; `SAMPLE=<category>/<sample>` installs one; `ACTION=undeploy` removes them. The full ladder is executed via `samples/acme-admin/demo-suite.sh` (optionally with `RUN_CHAT_LEG=true`); individual demos run from their own `demo/demo.sh`. Credentials are provisioned by `shared/platform-ops/gitops/sync-browser-credentials.sh dev-luban-aiops` before the app can start.
