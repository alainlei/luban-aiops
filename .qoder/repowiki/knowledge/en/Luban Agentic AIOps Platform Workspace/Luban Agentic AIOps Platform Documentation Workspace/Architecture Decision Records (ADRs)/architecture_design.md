Flat collection of numbered Markdown files under `docs/adr/`, each following the shared `template.md` structure (Status, Context, Decision, Alternatives Considered, Consequences) and governed by the conventions in `README.md`. Files are named `NNNN-<slug>.md` with sequential IDs; statuses are restricted to `proposed`, `accepted`, or `superseded by ADR-NNNN`. The index in `README.md` links every accepted ADR (`ADR-0001` through `ADR-0014`) and cross-references related specs via `SPEC-NNN` identifiers. ADRs act as a stable decision layer above the per-change specs in `docs/specs/`: they capture cross-product constraints, trust-model changes, technology selections, and reversals of earlier decisions, while local spec-level choices stay in the spec's own `plan.md`. Dependency direction is one-way — specs reference ADRs they depend on in their status header, but ADRs do not depend on live code.
