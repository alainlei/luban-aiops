Three cooperating layers with a clear hot-path / persistence split:

- `hitl_confirmations.py` — the in-process single-flight registry (`ConfirmationRegistry`, module singleton `CONFIRMATION_REGISTRY`) keyed by `session_id`. Holds `PendingConfirmation` dataclasses (tool calls, risk-level snapshots, gateway-name maps, browser flow context, change-request metadata) plus curated `build_change_request` formatters for mutating tools (`web.*`, `k8s.delete_pod`, `http.post`, `secrets.deliver`). Exposes `claim`/`resolve`/`take_for_expiry` to prevent double-resume of parked kernel replies.
- `flow_approvals.py` — two per-process stores mirroring gateway-owned state: `FlowContextStore` (`FLOW_CONTEXTS`) tracks the live `web.navigate` binding identity (`skill_id`+`origin`), and `FlowApprovalStore` (`FLOW_APPROVALS`) records the operator's first mutating-flow approval gated by TTL and identity match. Killing error codes (`BROWSER_REDIRECT_NOT_ALLOWED`, `BROWSER_FLOW_DENIED`, …) invalidate both stores atomically so auto-signing never outlives its binding.
- `confirmation_records.py` — durable backend layer behind the `ConfirmationRecordStore` Protocol with an `InMemoryConfirmationRecordStore` (dev/CI) and a `PostgresConfirmationRecordStore` (production, using `psycopg` + JSONB). A `build_confirmation_record_store()` factory selects the backend via `AGENT_STATE_STORE_BACKEND`/`AGENT_STATE_DB_URL`, runs startup DDL + stale-pending sweep, and exposes a module-level `CONFIRMATION_RECORD_STORE` singleton.

Dependency direction is one-way: `confirmation_records` depends on neither of the other two; `hitl_confirmations` and `flow_approvals` are independent hot paths consumed by the runtime kernel and v2 routes. Both in-memory registries are deliberately process-local and non-persistent — they fail safe (re-park) on restart rather than auto-running parked calls.
