`make build` generates coordinated `IMAGE_TAG`s saved to `.images.env`; `make deploy` applies the overlay, waits for rollouts, then runs secret-sync scripts. Provider selection is done via `select-runtime-profile.sh default` and verified with `verify-runtime-profile.sh`. Dashboards are validated offline with `make validate-dashboards` (or `python3 shared/platform-ops/dashboards/validate_dashboards.py`) and imported live with `OO_ROOT_USER_EMAIL=... OO_ROOT_USER_PASSWORD=... sh shared/platform-ops/dashboards/apply-dashboards.sh`. OIDC realm and browser client are reconciled by `reconcile-luban-realm.sh` and `reconcile-portal-oidc-client.sh` before deployment.
