`make deploy-sample-app` builds the acme-admin image, applies `deploy/`, then runs nine assertions including `/healthz` and authenticated `/api/users` checks; `make deploy-samples` installs all skill docs into the `skills-samples` ConfigMap; `SAMPLE=<category>/<sample>` deploys one; `ACTION=undeploy` removes the ConfigMap. Running demos requires port-forwarding `identity-service:18081` and `platform-gateway:18083`, and `RUN_CHAT_LEG=true` gates non-deterministic chat legs. Credentials are provisioned by `shared/platform-ops/gitops/sync-browser-credentials.sh dev-luban-aiops`.
