The repository is a product-oriented monorepo. `products/` holds independently buildable services (agent-platform, identity-broker, platform-gateway, tool-gateway, audit-service, incident-service, execution-runtime, skills-hub) plus the operator-portal SPA; each product ships its own `src/<package>/`, `tests/`, `Dockerfile`, `Makefile`, `pyproject.toml`, and `uv.lock`. Cross-cutting concerns live in `shared/shared-contracts` (API/event/policy schemas, SQL, policy bundles, validation scripts), `shared/shared-sdk` (client/auth/tracing helpers), and `shared/platform-ops` (Kubernetes/kustomize GitOps overlays under `gitops/` plus e2e demo scripts). `mk/` provides shared Makefile fragments (`defaults.mk`, `image.mk`, `python.mk`) that every product's Makefile includes. The root `Makefile` orchestrates cross-product workflows: `sync`/`test`/`lint`/`build`/`push` iterate over `PYTHON_PRODUCTS` and `IMAGE_PRODUCTS`; `verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate combining per-product tests, kustomize overlay rendering, canonical policy validation/scenario checks, version lockstep, secret-vocabulary validation, the local secret-delivery demo, portal Vitest + production build, and the crash-safety failure test. Deployment targets are GitOps overlays under `shared/platform-ops/gitops/` (dev-k8s, runtime-profiles/*); `make deploy` delegates to `gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh`. Samples (`samples/acme-admin/`) are installed out-of-band via `make deploy-samples` so they never enter the base overlay. Documentation follows an ADR/spec/release-note triad under `docs/adr/`, `docs/specs/SPEC-NNN/` (plan/spec/tasks), and `docs/agentic-aiops-platform/release-notes/`, with operator/user guides under `docs/guides/`.
