The workspace is an orchestrator rather than a runtime: `Makefile` is the single entry point that delegates per-product work to `products/<name>/Makefile` files while owning cross-cutting concerns — coordinated image tagging (single `IMAGE_TAG` derived from `VERSION`), policy bundle sync (`sync-policy` copies canonical policies under `shared/shared-contracts/policies/` into tool-gateway, platform-gateway, and the dev-k8s overlay), version lockstep validation, secret-vocabulary checks, Kustomize overlay rendering, OpenObserve dashboard validation, portal SPA testing, and the `e2e` demo suite. Product services (agent-platform, platform-gateway, execution-runtime, audit-service, identity-broker, incident-service, skills-hub, tool-gateway) share `shared/shared-contracts_and_sdk` for JSON Schema contracts, policy bundles, and skill format; they are all built as `luban-aiops/*` images tagged with the same semver+sha and deployed together via `shared/platform_ops/gitops`. Samples (`samples/acme-admin`) are intentionally kept out of the base overlay and installed separately through `make deploy-samples`, keeping the production overlay decoupled from tutorial content.
