The repository is a product-oriented monorepo: `products/` holds independently deployable services (agent-platform, platform-gateway, tool-gateway, identity-broker, skills-hub, audit-service, incident-service, execution-runtime, operator-portal), `shared/` holds cross-cutting assets (`shared-contracts` for API/event/policy schemas, `shared-sdk`, `platform-ops` for Kustomize overlays, dashboards, and e2e scripts), and `docs/` carries spec-driven development artifacts (`adr/`, `specs/SPEC-NNN/{plan,spec,tasks}`, release notes, guides, and workspace spikes). Each Python product is self-contained with its own `pyproject.toml`, `uv.lock`, `.python-version`, `Dockerfile`, `Makefile`, `src/<package>/`, and `tests/`. The root `Makefile` orchestrates cross-product routines — `sync`, `test`, `build`, `push`, `overlays`, `verify`, `deploy`, `e2e` — delegating per-product work via `make -C products/<name>` and sharing fragments from `mk/defaults.mk`, `mk/image.mk`, `mk/python.mk`. Policy bundles are authored once in `shared/shared-contracts/policies/` and synced into consumers via `make sync-policy`; versions across products and the portal are lockstep-checked by `make validate-version`. The operator-portal SPA is the only non-uv product and is exercised through `make portal-test` (vitest + tsc/vite) rather than `make test`.
