The workspace is an aggregation of independently deployable FastAPI services (agent_platform, audit_service, execution_runtime, identity_broker, incident_service, platform_gateway, skills_hub, tool_gateway) plus supporting layers (operator_portal SPA, platform_ops Kustomize overlays, documentation, samples). Cross-service communication is governed by shared JSON Schema contracts and a canonical YAML action-authorization policy bundle in shared_contracts, which every service validates against. The platform_gateway acts as the external edge, authenticating portal requests and proxying to internal services; operator_portal serves via nginx and proxies through the gateway. platform_ops provides the unified Kustomize-based deployment surface for all children, while documentation codifies the spec-driven development workflow that ties authoring, validation, and deployment together.
