Backend selection is controlled entirely by environment variables: `SESSION_STORE_BACKEND=memory|redis|postgres` (default `memory`), `SESSION_DB_URL` required when postgres is chosen, `SESSION_TTL_SECONDS` (default 3600), `SESSION_MAX_ENTRIES` (default 1000), and `SESSION_REDIS_HOST/PORT/DB`. Unknown backend values raise at startup; unreachable Postgres falls back to memory while recording a metric.
