The repository is a product-oriented monorepo: `products/<name>/` holds independently buildable services (agent-platform, platform-gateway, tool-gateway, identity-broker, audit-service, incident-service, execution-runtime, skills-hub, operator-portal), each with its own `pyproject.toml`, `Dockerfile`, `Makefile`, `src/<package>/`, and `tests/`. Cross-cutting assets live in `shared/`: `shared-contracts` defines API/event/policy schemas consumed by multiple products, `platform-ops` bundles Kubernetes GitOps overlays, dashboards, and e2e scripts, and `shared-sdk` provides client libraries. `docs/specs/SPEC-*` documents drive feature delivery via plan/spec/tasks triads, and `docs/adr/` records architectural decisions. The root `Makefile` orchestrates cross-product concerns — coordinated image tagging from `VERSION`, overlay rendering via kustomize, policy bundle sync/validation, dashboard validation, portal SPA build, and the `verify` pre-commit gate — while delegating per-product `sync/test/lint/build/push` to each product's Makefile. Dependency direction is inward: products depend on `shared/shared-contracts` and `shared/shared-sdk`; they do not import each other directly, communicating instead through the documented API/event boundaries.
