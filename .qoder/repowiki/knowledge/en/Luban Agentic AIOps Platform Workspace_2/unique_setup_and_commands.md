`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate running every product's pytest suite plus kustomize overlay rendering, dashboard/schema validation, policy bundle diffing, version lockstep, secret-vocabulary checks, portal unit tests + production build, and an execution-failure proof script. `make build` produces coordinated images tagged `<semver>-<prefix>[-<profile>]-<gitsha>` and writes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`; `AUTO_LOAD_KIND=true KIND_CLUSTER_NAME=<name>` auto-loads all images into kind. `make e2e` runs the scripted demos against a deployed cluster after port-forwarding platform-gateway on 18083 and identity-broker on 18081.
